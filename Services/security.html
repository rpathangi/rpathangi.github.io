AWS Methodology

AI-Driven Development Life Cycle

AI-DLC positions AI as a central collaborator across the software lifecycle — AI plans the work and asks clarifying questions, while humans make the critical decisions. It runs in three phases, Inception, Construction, and Operations, so security and quality are built in from the start rather than bolted on at the end.
