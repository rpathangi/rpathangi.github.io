Pre-Migration Questionnaire

A discovery checklist to prepare for migration and modernization.

Use these questions to gather the context AWS Transform and your migration team need before planning a move. Capture answers per application or workload; the more complete the discovery, the more accurate the assessment and business case.

Group 01

Business Drivers & Objectives

  • What business outcomes are driving the migration (cost, agility, resilience, end-of-support)?
  • What is the target timeline, and are there fixed deadlines such as a data-center exit or license renewal?
  • How will success be measured?
Group 02

Application Portfolio

  • How many applications are in scope, and how are they prioritized?
  • Which applications are business-critical or customer-facing?
  • What are the usage patterns and peak load periods?
Group 03

Architecture & Dependencies

  • What technology stacks, languages, and frameworks are in use?
  • What are the upstream and downstream integrations and data flows?
  • Are there shared services or tightly coupled components?
Group 04

Infrastructure & Environment

  • How many servers or VMs, and what operating systems and versions?
  • Where do workloads run today (on-premises, colocation, other cloud, VMware)?
  • What are the current compute, memory, and storage footprints?
Group 05

Data & Databases

  • What database engines, versions, and data volumes are involved?
  • What are the data growth rates and retention requirements?
  • Are there data residency or sovereignty constraints?
Group 06

Networking & Connectivity

  • What are the current bandwidth, latency, and connectivity requirements?
  • How are DNS, firewalls, and load balancing configured?
  • Is hybrid connectivity needed during the migration?
Group 07

Security & Compliance

  • Which regulatory or compliance frameworks apply?
  • What are the identity, access, and encryption requirements?
  • What audit, logging, and monitoring standards must be met?
Group 08

Licensing & Cost

  • What operating system, database, and third-party licenses are in scope?
  • Are any licenses bring-your-own (BYOL) eligible?
  • What is the current run-cost baseline?
Group 09

Operations, Resilience & Readiness

  • What are the RTO and RPO targets per application?
  • How are backup, disaster recovery, monitoring, and alerting handled today?
  • What is the team's cloud experience and preferred migration disposition (rehost, replatform, refactor, retire, retain)?
